Single-Use Coaxial / Bimanual I/A Instruments

Single-use coaxial I/A instruments

These are our WHITESTAR SIGNATURE™ PRO phaco system accessories. Our disposable irrigation/aspiration instruments offer:

  • Modern ergonomic design
  • Exceptional grip for outstanding handling
  • Colour coding of gauge sizes
  • Available for recommended incisions sizes of: 1.8 mm / 2.2 mm / 2.5 mm
  • Polished or textured tips

Ordering and Description

  • MCS4520P: 1 angled 45º 20G, asp. port 0.35 mm, handpick with polished asp. head incision size 2.5 mm
  • MCS6520T: 1 angled 65° 20 G, asp. port 0.35 mm, handpiece with textured asp. head, incision size 2.5 mm
  • MCS4521P: 1 angled 45° 21 G, asp. port 0.35 mm, handpiece with polished asp. head, incision size 2.2 mm
  • MCS4523P: 1 angled 45° 23 G, asp. port 0.30 mm, handpiece with polished asp. head, incision size 1.8 mm

Single-use bimanual I/A instruments

  • Modern ergonomic design
  • Exceptional grip for outstanding handling
  • Colour coding of gauge sizes
  • Available for recommended incisions sizes of: 0.8 mm / 1.1 mm

Ordering and Description

  • MBSCR21: 1 aspiration handpiece textured 21 G, 1 irrigation handpiece polished 21 G, with 2 irrigation ports 0.45 mm, incision size of 1.1 mm
  • MBSCR23: 1 aspiration handpiece textured 23 G, 1 irrigation handpiece polished 23 G, with 2 irrigation ports 0.35 mm, incision size of 0.8 mm
