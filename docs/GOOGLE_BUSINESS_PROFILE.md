## 11. Asking for reviews

After verification, copy the review link from the profile (**Ask for
reviews**) and send it to customers after a successful installation:

```
Thank you for choosing MP MedPharma. If you were happy with the equipment and the installation, a short Google review would help other practices find us: [review link]
```

Google forbids offering anything in return for a review (discounts, gifts)
and forbids asking only happy customers in a way that filters out others.
Send it to every completed customer.

## 12. Verification

Google will ask you to verify, usually by a short video showing the business
(signage or vehicle, equipment or workspace, and proof you manage it, such as
opening the business's tools or paperwork) or occasionally by phone or
postcard. Verification can take several days. The profile is not public until
it is verified.
